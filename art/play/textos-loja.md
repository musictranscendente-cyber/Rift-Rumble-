## Descrição completa (até 4000 caracteres)
Rift Rumble is a fast platform fighting game where zodiac warriors battle on floating arenas. There's no health bar: every hit raises your damage %, and the higher it goes, the farther you fly. Knock your opponents off the arena to win!

6 UNIQUE FIGHTERS – each with their own special move and a cinematic ultimate:
• Solkar ♌ – the solar lion king who falls from the sky like a burning sun
• Lunara ♋ – the tide guardian who becomes a giant sea creature
• Bront ♉ – the stone bull who raises pillars of rock and crystal
• Astrael ♐ – the star archer with a chargeable comet arrow
• Noxara ♏ – the scorpion assassin who grabs, poisons and strikes from the shadows
• Vex ♊ – the twins of light and shadow who teleport and summon dragons

6 ARENAS with different layouts: ramps, high platforms, wide open stages and more.

FEATURES
• Quick matches: tap PLAY and fight in seconds
• Up to 4 fighters on screen against smart bots (3 difficulty levels)
• Stock (3 lives) or timed matches
• Player levels, daily missions and streak rewards
• Hand-drawn anime style with smooth video-based animations
• Simple touch controls: joystick, attack, special, jump, shield and ultimate
• Plays offline – no internet needed
• No ads, no account, no data collected

Pick your fighter, fill your ultimate bar and launch everyone off the rift!
